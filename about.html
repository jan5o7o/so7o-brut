SO7O BRUT: A Neo-Brutalism-Inspired Typeface. Born from the raw, structural honesty of neo-brutalist web and graphic design, SO7O BRUT strips typography down to blunt geometry. Every glyph in its 102-character unicase set is constructed from hard 90-degree edges and intentional 45-degree chamfers—zero Bézier curves, zero apologies. Whether set tight in Condensed for stark poster headlines or stretched out in Wide for high-impact UI banners, it brings industrial heft and unmistakable character to modern digital layouts.

How it is made

one image, then geometry

The T, E, A and M skeletons were measured from a single wordmark (assets/team-source.jpg) and scaled into a 700-unit cap height. Everything else — the rest of the alphabet, the digits, the punctuation, the light cut — was drawn on those same rules: monolithic slabs, rectangular punched windows, straight lines only.

Lowercase is mapped onto the capitals, so the face sets unicase. There is one weight per cut and no italic; the Light cut is the Slab skeleton inset by 22 units with the cap and baseline anchored.
